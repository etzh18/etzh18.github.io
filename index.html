“Messi” Business: The Effect of Clean Zones, Crowds and Closures at the 2026 World Cup

Sole author, with Cornell University & EXEA Labs. Ongoing.

FIFA had host cities set up “Clean Zones” that limit signs and street vendors near stadiums and fan fests. I'm checking whether those rules hurt local businesses in all 16 host cities. So far it looks like they didn't: sales near the zones held up, and crowds and spending went up. Working paper (PDF)
